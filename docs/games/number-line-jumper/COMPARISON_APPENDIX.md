# Number Line Jumper — Comparison Appendix

This appendix is the durable comparator registry for GAME-222. It promotes
Motion Math Zoom into the named comparison set and gives every comparator a
dimension and a Meets bar. The labels describe the evidence available to this
repository; they do not certify the current behavior of an external product.

## Evidence labels

- `verified-here`: a route or artifact was directly captured and checked in
  with enough identity to reproduce the observation.
- `described-mechanic-only`: the comparator is part of the product discussion,
  but no current route/artifact was captured in this repository. This label
  cannot support a Meets or Exceeds claim about the comparator.

All six comparators are currently `described-mechanic-only`. The repository
does not claim that a black-canvas route, an unpinned page, or a remembered
mechanic is verified evidence.
